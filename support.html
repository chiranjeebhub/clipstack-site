Clipstack: Clipboard History for Mac

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub and it will be answered as soon as possible.

Getting started

  • Press ⇧⌘V (or the shortcut you set in Settings › General) to open your clipboard history.
  • Use ↑ ↓ to choose an item and Return to paste it. ⌥Return pastes as plain text.
  • Use ← → to switch between All, Text, Links, Images and Files, and ⇧⌘P to show pinned items.
  • ⌘1–9 pastes one of the first nine items directly, and ⌘P pins or unpins the selected item.

Paste doesn’t work, the item is only copied

Clipstack needs Accessibility access to paste into other apps. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility and turn on Clipstack. If it’s already on, turn it off and on again after an update.

iCloud sync

Turn it on in Settings › Storage. Your Mac must be signed in to iCloud. History, pinned items and settings then appear on your other Macs and come back after a reinstall.

Passwords and private apps

Items marked as passwords are never saved. To stop recording from a specific app, add it in Settings › Ignored Apps.
