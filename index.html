Clipstack

Clipboard history for Mac

Clipstack keeps everything you copy (text, links, code, colors, images and files) and brings it back with one shortcut (⇧⌘V). Search your history, pin the things you use often, and paste straight into the app you’re working in.

Private by design. Your history stays on your Mac. If you turn on iCloud sync, it goes only to your own private iCloud account. Clipstack has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.
