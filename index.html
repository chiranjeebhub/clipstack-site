Features

Small app. Big habit.

Built in SwiftUI and AppKit, so it feels like part of macOS and stays out of your way until you call it.

Quick panel

Floats over whatever you’re doing without stealing focus. Search as you type and filter by text, links, images, files or pins.

Pastes for you

Press return and the item lands in the app you were using. ⌥↩ strips formatting, and ⌘1–9 pastes the top nine instantly.

Pins that stay

Pin addresses, snippets and replies you reuse. History limits never remove them, and they sit in the menu bar with ⌥1–9.

Rich previews

Text, code, links, images, files and colors each get a proper preview. Fix a typo in a copied snippet with ⌘E.

iCloud sync, if you want it

Turn it on and your history, pins, settings and shortcut follow you to your other Macs and come back after a reinstall.

Knows where it came from

Every item shows the app it was copied from, so you can find that link from Safari or that line from Terminal at a glance.

Privacy

Your clipboard is nobody’s business.

Clipboards hold passwords, addresses and private messages. Clipstack is built so that the only person who ever sees them is you.

Read the privacy policy →

  • Stays on your MacHistory is stored locally. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you switch on iCloud sync, which uses your own private iCloud database.
  • Skips passwordsItems that password managers mark as concealed or transient are never saved.
  • Ignore apps, pause anytimeList apps whose copies are never recorded, and pause recording from the menu bar.
  • No accounts, analytics or adsNo sign-in, no third-party SDKs, and the code is open for anyone to check.

Questions

Good to know.

Something else? Ask on the support page.

Why does Clipstack need Accessibility access?

macOS only lets an app send ⌘V to other apps once you allow Accessibility. Clipstack uses it for that one job and never reads your keystrokes or screen. Without it, choosing an item copies it and you paste it yourself.

Will it save my passwords?

No. Password managers mark what they copy as concealed or transient, and Clipstack skips those items. You can also add any app to Ignored Apps.

Can I change the shortcut?

Yes. ⇧⌘V is the default, and you can record any shortcut you like in Settings.

Does it sync between my Macs?

Yes, if you turn on iCloud sync. History, pins, settings and your shortcut sync through your own private iCloud database. It’s off by default.

How much history does it keep?

You choose how long items are kept and the maximum number of items. Pinned items are never removed by those limits.
